## Why choose a lifetime license?

Subscriptions add up. A UI kit is a tool you use on project after project, so paying once often makes more sense:

- **Lower long-term cost.** A $199 lifetime license costs less than two years of most subscriptions.
- **No lost access.** Your projects never depend on keeping a plan active.
- **Future releases.** Good lifetime plans include new blocks as they ship.
- **Client work.** Agencies and freelancers can reuse the same kit across many client sites.

## How do the other lifetime kits compare?

### Tailwind Plus

Tailwind Plus, from the makers of Tailwind CSS, is a one-time purchase with lifetime access to all current and future component packages and site templates. It costs $299 for one person or $979 for a team of up to 25. It is the benchmark for quality, but its components are copy-paste markup rather than shadcn registry installs, and it costs $100 more than Pasta UI Pro for one person.

### Magic UI Pro

Magic UI Pro costs $199 once and adds more than 50 animated landing sections and several templates to the free Magic UI library. Great for launches that lean on motion.

### Aceternity UI Pro

Aceternity UI Pro offers more than 200 premium blocks and templates with bold effects. Lifetime access is $199, with a $169 annual option and a $1,590 team plan for 10 people.

### shadcnblocks

shadcnblocks sells one of the largest shadcn block catalogs with lifetime updates: $149 for Pro, $299 for Premium with templates and a Figma kit, and $399 for Elite with pre-built pages and a page builder. All plans are single user.

### Shadcn Studio

Shadcn Studio offers lifetime plans from $99 for one person, with a $199 Pro plan that adds a builder and Figma kit, plus team plans. It also has a free community tier.

### Tailark

Tailark has a free tier and one-time plans at $249, $299 with page templates, and $499 for teams of up to 10.

### Untitled UI React

Untitled UI React's Pro Solo license is $349 once, with team plans from $999. It pairs with the Untitled UI Figma kit, which is sold separately.

### Cruip

Cruip sells complete Tailwind landing page templates. Its Classic bundle is $79 and its Modern bundle is $149, both one-time with lifetime updates for included templates.

## What should you check before buying a lifetime license?

1. **What "lifetime" covers.** Does it include future releases, or only today's catalog? Pasta UI Pro includes every future release.
2. **Seats.** Most kits are per person. Teams usually need a team plan or several licenses.
3. **Client work.** Confirm you can use the kit on client projects. Pasta UI Pro allows it.
4. **Resale.** Kits generally forbid reselling the components as a kit of your own.
5. **Install method.** A shadcn registry install saves time on every project.
6. **Fit with your stack.** If you use React, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui, choose a kit built for that stack.

## Is an annual plan ever better?

Yes, if you need a kit for one project and plan to finish within a year. Pasta UI Pro's annual plan costs $149, includes updates while it is active and renews once a year until you cancel. You can cancel any time and keep access until the end of the period you paid for. If you build sites regularly, the $199 lifetime plan, a single payment with no renewal, pays for itself quickly.
